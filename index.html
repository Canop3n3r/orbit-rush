How to fly
  • Space ignite, then stage   Shift/Ctrl (or ↑/↓) throttle   Z/X full/cut
  • W/S pitch · A/D yaw · Q/E roll (gimbal, reaction wheels, RCS). SAS holds attitude: 1 stability 2 prograde 3 retrograde 4/5 radial out/in 6/7 normal/anti · V SAS on/off
  • C RCS on/off · I/K J/L H/N RCS translate (with an RCS ring)
  • F AUTO flies to orbit: gravity turn, Max-Q throttling, staging, coast, circularize. Steer or press F to take over.
  • T / , warp (above 140 km while coasting = exact Kepler rails) · G warp to apoapsis · M map · B booster cam · Y fly the booster · R hangar
  • Full-scale Earth: orbit is ~7.8 km/s and costs ~9.4 km/s of Δv. Pitch over gently east after liftoff, keep the angle of attack small through Max-Q, cut at Ap ~180 km, then burn prograde at Ap until Pe > 130 km.
  • Coming home: build a capsule (pod + heat shield under it, parachute pack on top, a separator below; try the Orbit & home template). In orbit press O: AUTO turns retrograde, burns Pe down to ~45 km, drops the service stage and flies the capsule heat-shield first. P deploys parachutes by hand: drogues below Mach 1.2 and 15 kPa, mains under 95 m/s — outside those limits they shred. Watch the HEAT gauges: every part has a temperature limit.
  • U cycles effects quality (High/Medium/Low). / opens the Learn overlay: what every HUD readout means (or hover a label).
  • New to rockets? Open the 🎓 Academy: twelve short lessons from a first hop to a Moon transfer.
  • Keep the yellow CoM above the cyan CoP (fins at the bottom) or the rocket flips. Stage with fuel left in the booster and it can land on the droneship (+1000 auto, +2500 by hand with Y).
