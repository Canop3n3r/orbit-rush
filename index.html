ORBIT RUSH

Build it. Launch it. Orbit it. Land it.

How to fly
  • Space ignite / stage   W/S throttle   Z/X full/cut
  • A/D pitch   F AUTO (gravity turn + Max-Q throttle + cutoff)   M map   B booster cam   T / , warp up/down   G warp to apoapsis   L fly the booster   R back to hangar
  • Ignite, then press F for AUTO-TURN (or fly the gravity turn yourself with A/D). Throttle back (S) when it says HIGH Q.
  • Cut engines when apoapsis (Ap) > 75 km, coast up, then burn prograde near Ap until periapsis (Pe) > 70 km = ORBIT.
  • Stage above 2 km with fuel left in the booster: it auto-lands for +1000, or press L to fly the landing yourself (W/S throttle, A/D tilt, F hold retrograde) for +2500 plus accuracy and softness bonus. The upper stage flies itself meanwhile.
