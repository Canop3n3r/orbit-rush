How to fly
  • Space ignite; Space twice to stage   W/S throttle   Z/X full/cut   A/D pitch
  • F AUTO flies to orbit: gravity turn, Max-Q, staging when a stage is empty, coast, circularize. Steer or press F to take over.
  • T / , warp · G warp to apoapsis · M map · B booster cam · L fly the booster · R back to hangar
  • Manual: cut engines when apoapsis (Ap) > 75 km, coast up, then burn prograde near Ap until periapsis (Pe) > 70 km = ORBIT.
  • Stage 1 above 2 km with fuel left and it can land on the droneship (+1000 auto, +2500 by hand with L).
