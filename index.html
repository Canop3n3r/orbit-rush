ORBIT RUSH

Build it. Launch it. Orbit it. Land it.

How to fly
  • Space ignite; Space twice to stage   W/S throttle   Z/X full/cut
  • A/D pitch   F AUTO (flies all the way to orbit: gravity turn, Max-Q, coast, circularize)   M map   B booster cam   T / , warp up/down   G warp to apoapsis   L fly the booster   R back to hangar
  • Ignite, then press F for AUTO — it climbs, coasts to apoapsis and circularizes by itself (press G to warp the coast). Or fly it yourself with A/D and throttle back (S) when it says HIGH Q.
  • Manual: cut engines when apoapsis (Ap) > 75 km, coast up, then burn prograde near Ap until periapsis (Pe) > 70 km = ORBIT.
  • Stage (Space twice) above 2 km with fuel left in the booster (the hangar tells you how much it needs): it auto-lands for +1000, or press L to fly the landing yourself (W/S throttle, A/D tilt, F hold retrograde) for +2500 plus accuracy and softness bonus. The upper stage flies itself meanwhile.
