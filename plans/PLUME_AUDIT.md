# Plume audit (rockets-pass-1, unit 3)

Generated by `node tools/plume_audit.mjs` from the models' own bells (`gfx/vehicles/nozzles.js`) and the roster (`fun/exhaust.js`). Do not edit by hand.

## How a plume is built

- **Look** is data: `fun/plumetable.js` `PLUME_TABLE`, one row per propellant:
  - **kerolox**: orange-yellow, Mach diamonds, sooty trail
  - **methalox**: pale blue, clean, faint steam
  - **hydrolox**: nearly transparent blue-white
  - **hypergolic**: faint, pale peach
  - **solid**: bright orange-white, thick smoke trail
- **Engine count, position, size** per stage: the model's bells (`plumeLayout`), so a plume sits under every visible bell. Vernier-size bells (< 45 % of the biggest) get no main plume.
- **Vacuum vs sea level**: an engine with no sea-level thrust is a vacuum nozzle (wider bell, fewer Mach diamonds). **Thrust scaling**: length and width grow with sqrt(per-engine thrust), clamped.
- **Side boosters** keep their own radial plumes (one per booster nozzle). `*` = propellant guessed (no roster data).

## Known gaps (follow-ups)

- **NO-ROSTER** models: engine count, layout and vacuum nozzles come from the model, but the propellant is a guess (kerolox, solid for strap-ons) because the roster has no stage data for them. Fix = add roster stages (sourced), not art.
- **COUNT** models: the plume follows what the model shows; where the model shows fewer or more bells than the roster lists engines, the model art should be revisited (unit 4).
- **Side boosters / strap-ons** keep one plume per roster booster engine placed at the booster centre (`alignRadialPlumes`); they do not yet follow per-bell positions. Models whose SRB group has no nozzle at all (STAGE-WITHOUT-BELLS) need a nozzle in the art.
- **Ullage / RCS plumes** are not modelled yet (no thruster plumes, no ullage puff at separation).
- Vacuum-only engines widen the bell and drop Mach diamonds; a true sea-level to vacuum plume morph per engine (expansion ratio) is approximated by `fxmap.plumeShape` from ambient pressure only.
